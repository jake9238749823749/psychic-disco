# AI Writing Tells — Sweep Before Publishing

These patterns read as machine-generated because of *frequency*, not because any single one is banned. Sweep drafts against this list; when a pattern genuinely serves the sentence, keep it. The goal is prose that reads authored, not prose that reads sanitized.

## Words and stock phrases (cut or replace)

- delve, tapestry, landscape (metaphorical), navigate (metaphorical), journey (metaphorical), robust, seamless(ly), leverage (verb), utilize, elevate, unlock, supercharge, game-changing, revolutionize
- "In today's fast-paced world / ever-evolving landscape" — delete the sentence entirely
- "It's important to note that" / "It's worth mentioning" — say the thing or don't

## Constructions

- "It's not just X — it's Y." / "This isn't about X; it's about Y." Once per document, maximum.
- Rule-of-three everywhere ("clear, concise, and compelling"). Vary list lengths.
- Em-dash chains — like this — in every sentence — vary punctuation instead.
- A bolded topic phrase at the start of every bullet.
- Every paragraph the same length; every section symmetric. Real writing is lopsided where it matters.
- Rhetorical question as a transition? Rarely.

## Tone

- Exclamation-mark enthusiasm; "Certainly!", "Great question!", "Absolutely!"
- Conclusions that restate everything, or open with "In conclusion."
- Hedges stacked for safety ("could potentially, in some cases, possibly").
- Compliment-sandwich feedback when the middle is the only part that matters.

## The positive test

After sweeping, read one paragraph aloud. If every sentence has the same rhythm and length, the sweep removed the tells but not the tell-tale cadence — vary sentence length until it sounds like a person.
